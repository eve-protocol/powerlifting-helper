# Volume Block v6

4-week accumulation block kept close to volume_block_v5: 3 squat exposures (2 low-bar, 1 high-bar), 4 bench exposures (paused, incline DB, competition bench, Spoto), and 2 deadlift exposures while avoiding extra lower-back fatigue. Week 1 re-enters with controlled 8s, then 6s, 5-rep prep, and week-4 5RM benchmarks. Face pulls removed; pull-ups stay twice weekly.

## Overview

- Weeks: 4
- Days per week: 5
- Total workouts: 20
