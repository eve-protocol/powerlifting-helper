# Strength Block v6

Three-week athlete-specific intensification block that preserves meaningful rep work. Week 1 uses competition-lift fours without singles, Week 2 uses one deliberately submaximal single before triples, and Week 3 tests objective triple benchmarks without a calibration single. Primary low-bar squat and competition bench are consolidated on Wednesday, while Friday remains a written condo accessory day. High bar, paused bench, incline dumbbells, Spoto, twice-weekly pull-ups, hack squat support work, backoff work, and paused sumo at the top edge of the kneecap remain in place.

## Overview

- Weeks: 3
- Days per week: 5
- Total workouts: 15

**Table guide:** Sets are the original set numbers; identical consecutive sets share a row (e.g. 2–4). Reps are per set. Range kg is the daily selection band; (start) marks a suggested starting anchor, and (example) marks a backoff anchor to recalculate from the actual top triple. — means no value is prescribed.

## Program Structure

### Week 1

#### Day 1 — Monday - High Bar / Paused Bench / Pull-Ups / Belt Squat

**Day intent:** Retain rep strength and developmental squat work while introducing the belt squat away from the combined primary day.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps | RPE | Anchor kg |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| High Bar Squat (Barbell) | 1–3 | 5 | 6.5–7 | 125 |
| Bench Press (Paused) | 1–3 | 4 | 7–7.5 | 110 |
| Pull-Up (Weighted) | 1–3 | 5 | 7.5–8 | 27.5 |
| Belt Squat | 1–2 | 10 | 6.5–7 | 40 |

**Why these movements:**

- **High Bar Squat (Barbell):** Keep knee-forward quad development active through intensification.
- **Bench Press (Paused):** Keep long-pause bench volume active as competition work intensifies.
- **Pull-Up (Weighted):** Progress weighted pull-up strength without compromising the next primary lift.
- **Belt Squat:** Replace the poor leg-extension setup with low-axial-load quadriceps work; calibrate the new machine conservatively.

#### Day 2 — Tuesday - Paused Sumo at Knee / Incline DB / Supported Row / Cable Crunch

**Day intent:** Train the deadlift transition, progress incline dumbbells, and maintain supported upper-back strength, then use the open support slot for low-residual-fatigue trunk work.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps | RPE | Anchor kg |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sumo Deadlift (Paused) | 1–3 | 4 | 6–6.5 | 140 |
| Incline Bench Press (Dumbbell) | 1–3 | 6 | 7–7.5 | 50 |
| Seated Row (Cable) | 1–3 | 10 | 7–7.5 | 50.5 |
| Cable Crunch | 1–3 | 12 | 7–7.5 | 45 |

**Why these movements:**

- **Sumo Deadlift (Paused):** Pause when the bar center reaches the top edge of the kneecap, hold position, then finish with hips through rather than leaning back.
- **Incline Bench Press (Dumbbell):** Progress incline dumbbell strength as a primary bench outcome.
- **Seated Row (Cable):** Maintain upper-back strength with torso support and less lower-back fatigue than the prior standing row.
- **Cable Crunch:** Maintain trunk strength without interfering with primary lifts.

- **Rear Delt Fly (Cable):** Maintain rear-delt work on the available cable station; adjust leverage before forcing a poor stack increment.
