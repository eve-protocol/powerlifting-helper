# Peaking Block v8

Three-week athlete-specific realization block. Week 1 expresses heavier singles while retaining doubles and controlled incline work. Tuesday uses easy competition sumo practice rather than paused variations. Week 2 places the final heavy squat and bench ten days before the mock meet and the final heavy deadlift seven days before it. Weeks 1-2 retain a written low-fatigue Friday condo accessory day; Week 3 reduces volume substantially and keeps mock-meet-week Friday as full rest. No @9.5 single and no repeated proof attempt.

## Overview

- Weeks: 3
- Days per week: 5
- Total workouts: 14

**Table guide:** Sets are the original set numbers; identical consecutive sets share a row (e.g. 2–4). Reps are per set. Range kg is the daily selection band; (start) marks a suggested starting anchor, and (example) marks a backoff anchor to recalculate from the actual top triple. — means no value is prescribed.

## Program Structure

### Week 1

#### Day 1 — Monday - Specific Squat / Competition Bench / Pull-Ups

**Day intent:** Retain useful rep work while reducing fatigue and increasing competition specificity; avoid quad soreness before Wednesday.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps | RPE | Anchor kg |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Squat (Low Bar) | 1–2 | 3 | 6–6.5 | 145 |
| Bench Press (Barbell) | 1–3 | 3 | 6.5–7 | 115 |
| Pull-Up (Weighted) | 1–2 | 5 | 7–7.5 | 25 |

**Why these movements:**

- **Squat (Low Bar):** Keep competition-squat positions active without adding a second heavy squat day.
- **Bench Press (Barbell):** Practise the exact competition touch, legal pause, and press with controlled triples; no extended counted pause.
- **Pull-Up (Weighted):** Progress weighted pull-up strength without compromising the next primary lift.

#### Day 2 — Tuesday - Competition Sumo / Incline DB / Supported Row / Cable Crunch

**Day intent:** Practise competition sumo and retain incline strength and supported upper-back work at controlled effort without compromising Wednesday's heavy squat and bench.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps | RPE | Anchor kg |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sumo Deadlift (Barbell) | 1–2 | 2 | 6.5–7 | 150 |
| Incline Bench Press (Dumbbell) | 1–2 | 5 | 7–7.5 | 52.5 |
| Seated Row (Cable) | 1–2 | 8 | 7–7.5 | 50.5 |
| Cable Crunch | 1–2 | 12 | 7–7.5 | 45 |

**Why these movements:**

- **Sumo Deadlift (Barbell):** Two easy competition-stance doubles with a connected, stacked lockout; no pauses or added top sets before Wednesday.
- **Incline Bench Press (Dumbbell):** Maintain primary incline dumbbell strength without fatigue spillover into Wednesday competition bench; only two controlled sets.
- **Seated Row (Cable):** Maintain supported upper-back strength without adding torso or lower-back fatigue.
- **Cable Crunch:** Maintain trunk work during realization.

#### Day 3 — Wednesday - Heavy Low Bar / Competition Bench

**Day intent:** Perform the heavy squat and bench exposures in meet order, with no accessory work between them; cap the session at two support movements afterward.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps | RPE | Anchor kg |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Squat (Low Bar) | 1 | 1 | 8–8.5 | 172.5 |
| Squat (Low Bar) | 2–3 | 2 | 7.5–8 | 162.5 |
| Bench Press (Barbell) | 1 | 1 | 8–8.5 | 140 |
| Bench Press (Barbell) | 2–3 | 2 | 7.5–8 | 130 |
| Pull-Up (Weighted) | 1–2 | 4 | 7–7.5 | 27.5 |
| Leg Curl | 1–2 | 8 | 7–7.5 | 30 |

**Why these movements:**

- **Squat (Low Bar):** Make 172.5kg look cleaner than the prior @9.5 result, then reinforce position with doubles.
- **Bench Press (Barbell):** Make 140kg a controlled heavy single with a handoff when available, then reinforce doubles.
- **Pull-Up (Weighted):** Progress weighted pull-up strength without compromising the next primary lift.
- **Leg Curl:** Maintain hamstring work without soreness spillover.

#### Day 4 — Friday - Condo Accessories / Fun

**Day intent:** Retain a written Friday session using low-fatigue chest, shoulders, arms, and trunk work while protecting Saturday deadlift and avoiding proof-of-readiness work.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps | RPE | Anchor kg |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chest Fly (Machine) | 1–2 | 10 | 7–7.5 | 35 |
| Rear Delt Fly (Dumbbell) | 1–2 | 15 | 7–7.5 | 7 |
| Lateral Raise (Cable) | 1–2 | 10 | 7–7.5 | 10 |
| Tricep Pushdown (Cable) | 1–2 | 8 | 7–7.5 | 55 |
| Bicep Curl (Dumbbell) | 1–2 | 8 | 7–7.5 | 11.5 |

**Why these movements:**

- **Chest Fly (Machine):** Use the verified Boostcamp entry for the condo cable-crossover fly setup with controlled, pain-free pec work.
- **Rear Delt Fly (Dumbbell):** Use a chest-supported dumbbell setup for smooth rear-delt loading without cable-stack jumps or residual lower-back fatigue.
- **Lateral Raise (Cable):** Maintain shoulder work on the available cable station without chasing stack jumps.
- **Tricep Pushdown (Cable):** Use the rope attachment for supported, pain-free triceps work; reduce immediately after reaching the RPE ceiling.
- **Bicep Curl (Dumbbell):** Use seated dumbbell curls to prevent body English and maintain pain-free elbow-flexor work; do not use the uncomfortable preacher setup.

#### Day 5 — Saturday - Heavy Sumo / Light Low Bar / Bench

**Day intent:** Express a clean heavy deadlift while retaining controlled backoff doubles.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps | RPE | Anchor kg |

| Squat (Low Bar) | 1–2 | 2 | 5.5–6 | 145 |
| Bench Press (Barbell) | 1–2 | 2 | 6–6.5 | 122.5 |

**Why these movements:**

- **Sumo Deadlift (Barbell):** Make 195kg a legal stacked single; floor speed alone does not pass the set.
- **Squat (Low Bar):** Maintain low-fatigue squat specificity after deadlift.
- **Bench Press (Barbell):** Maintain competition-bench frequency with low-fatigue doubles.
