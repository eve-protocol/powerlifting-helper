# Volume Block v5

4-week accumulation block inspired by accumulation/transmutation/realization periodization. Easier post-mock-meet re-entry, high-bar squat emphasis, x8-x10 bench/squat volume, Tuesday deadlift to protect Monday recovery, pull-ups twice weekly, and machine chest/rear-delt work instead of face pulls.

## Overview

- Weeks: 4
- Days per week: 5
- Total workouts: 20
