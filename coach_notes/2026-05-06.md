## Coaching assessment
- Squat load selection was good for the target: 130kg stayed within the intended RPE range for the first two sets.
- The missed rep on set 3 was a counting issue, not a capacity failure. The extra single made the total work effectively close enough; no replacement set was needed.
- Best technical finding: cueing `chest into the bar out of the hole` improved torso/hip timing and made Ren feel more quad contribution.
- Usual `knees out` form looked stronger and more natural, but it shifted the squat back toward the default posterior-chain pattern: hips out, shins more vertical, glutes/adductors/lower back taking over.
- The best stance today was the moderate compromise stance: enough room to hit depth, not wide enough to fully revert to hips-back low bar.
- Depth was acceptable across the work sets. Future focus should not be chasing more depth; it should be keeping tension and preventing hips from winning the first inch.

## Technical theme to carry forward
- Do not replace `knees out` with `knees forward only`; that distorted the descent and created diagonal bar travel.
- Preferred cue stack for low bar right now: `knees out and slightly forward`, then `chest with hips` / `chest into the bar` out of the hole.
- Avoid cueing `dip hips straight down` at the bottom. That caused loss of wedge/tension.

## Accessory notes
- Leg extension: 55kg was fine for set 1 but too high by set 2. 50kg is the better default when done after squat volume.
- T-bar row: 50kg is the right starting load, but bracing/fatigue made set 3 overshoot. Keep 50kg until all sets land cleanly inside target.
- Leg curl: 32.5kg overshot badly. Use 30kg as default until 3x12 is stable around RPE 7-7.5.

## Next time this pattern appears
- Start from the same moderate squat stance.
- Use squat shoes.
- Main cue: `chest into the bar out of the hole`.
- Secondary cue only if needed: `knees out and slightly forward`.
- Progress load only if the chest/hip timing survives later reps; otherwise repeat load and improve position.
