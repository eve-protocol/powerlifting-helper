## Coaching assessment
- Bench strength is still clearly present. 120kg x1 felt around @5.5-6 in warm-ups, and 130kg was available, but 130kg landed @8-8.5 instead of the programmed @7-7.5.
- The correct interpretation is not weakness. The top single was an intentional correction after the morning load-call undershot badly, but it overshot the prescription and should be treated as an informative exposure, not the new Week 1 default.
- The backoff call was mostly right for the first set: 117.5kg x4 @7.5 matched the target. Repeating 117.5kg overshot the second backoff to @8.5, so the better second-set call after an @8.5 single would have been 115kg x4.
- Pull-ups were strong but again drifted high by the final set. +25kg is validated for 5s, but not for all three sets if the cap is @8.5.
- Chest fly repeated the same accessory pattern from Volume Block v6: first set on target, second set above target. 80kg remains the safer repeated-set load when the goal is @7-7.5.
- Triceps were managed well after the second set reached target. Dropping to 25kg kept the third set productive.
- Curls overshot hard on the second set and missed target reps. 22.5kg is usable for one set, but 20kg is the better repeated-set load for 10s.

## Block trajectory impact
- Strength Block v5 is still on track, but Ren's read is valid: Week 1 is spicy. Four consecutive training days with exploratory loads, posterior-chain fatigue, and several accessory overshoots are making the entry week feel heavier than intended.
- Bench itself is not the concern. The concern is load-selection discipline and whether the block is accumulating too much local/systemic fatigue before the heavier weeks.
- The lower-body programming question remains live. Today's lower-back soreness after yesterday's low-bar session adds another data point that skipped high-bar/knee-dominant patterning may be allowing squat stress to drift back toward hips/lower back. It is not proof yet, but it should be monitored closely.
- Because Saturday's session will likely move to Sunday evening, the extra day before heavy deadlift is useful. Do not treat the schedule shift as a reason to overshoot; use it to let the posterior chain settle and keep Week 1 from compounding further.

## Technical theme to carry forward

- Accessories: first-set target does not justify repeating the same load if the second set historically jumps 1-2 RPE.
- Lower-body note: keep watching whether the absence of high-bar/knee-forward work makes low-bar stress feel more back-dominant.
