## Why no prescribed training singles?

No one-repetition **training work sets** remain, including Saturday light squat; competition attempts remain singles. Normal warm-up choices are not extra performance tests. The purpose is rep-led heavy specificity plus sufficient productive work, not a claim that singles cannot work or are inherently excessively fatiguing.

The researched Perkins example is **squat-specific**, not an all-lift/lifetime ban: Joe Stanek describes squat rep-led preparation but also primary bench singles. Borrow the principle of fitting the rep scheme to the athlete/lift, not Perkins' entire high-frequency schedule or competition lead-in. The scientific comparisons do not establish a requirement for singles against dose-comparable heavy doubles/triples plus backoffs.

- Joe Stanek, *Coaching The GOAT* (bench ~25:46–26:01, squat ~53:08): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4-wg0tjLrEM

## Evaluation and specific boundaries

- RPE, pain response and legal positions outrank anchors, percentages and expected tonnage. No mandatory upper endpoint or added single to prove the double/triple is available.
- A first work-set overshoot calls for a lower remaining load, not an immediate cycle rewrite. Repeated effort/position deterioration across comparable sessions warrants a small dose adjustment; isolated ordinary variability does not establish accumulated fatigue.
- Protect Wednesday squat: RDL is the first support work removed if Tuesday produces meaningful soreness/poorer squat positions. Do not increase RDL and paused-sumo loads to compensate for an easy day. Keep Wednesday bench low-cost rather than allowing it to become a second heavy bench progression.
- Evaluate whether matched rep work becomes more repeatable at the intended RPE and whether primary doubles retain legal positions. A better-looking single is not a prerequisite or an independent extra success test.
- Confirm federation/weigh-in logistics and existing custom-exercise account resolution before activation. No new names were invented in this revision. Program approval and training-state/Boostcamp activation remain separate actions.
