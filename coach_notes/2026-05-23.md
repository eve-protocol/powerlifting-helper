- Deadlift did not meet the programmed 3x5 @7.5-8 on paper, but the reason matters. Sets 1-2 were grip/bar-limited and included missed lockouts plus a low-value extra attempt. Set 3 with straps completed 170kg x5 but landed @9 after the prior grip-limited fatigue.
- Do not read today as "170kg is too heavy." Read it as "170kg is not a clean baseline when this bar is used without straps." For future sessions on the hard chrome ONI bar, straps should be the default for work sets; on the usual ONI bar, keep pulling without straps unless grip is actually the planned limiter.

- Larsen/bench: after an easy first set, take the smallest useful jump or repeat. Do not turn a secondary press slot into an @8.5 set unless the target allows it.
