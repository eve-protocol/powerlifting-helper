## Coaching assessment
- This was a major positive squat result. The programmed target was 1 x 1 @7.5-8, 1 x 3 @9-9.5, then 1 x 3 @7-7.5. The single was intentionally used as a low-fatigue readiness check, and the 165kg triple landed exactly at the top of the target range.
- The result meets the Strength Block v5 low-bar goal: 165kg x3 @9-9.5. It is especially meaningful because Ren did it during a poor-sleep work week and after several 160kg+ exposures felt more intimidating than they were.
- The technical caveat remains important. Rep 3 confirmed the old pattern under fatigue: knees retreat, hips move back, and the torso/back finish the rep. This was not a reason to reject the result, but it is a reason not to chase 167.5kg today and not to treat load alone as the success metric.
- The 152.5kg backoff was the correct call. Ren initially thought 155kg x3 might still be in range, but 152.5kg x3 already landed at @7.5 and supplied enough work without turning the backoff into another heavy-pattern rehearsal.
- Accessories were well controlled compared with several prior days. Leg extension and T-bar row hit the target effort exactly. Leg curl overshot reps on the first set but stayed inside effort and dropped to an easier second set, so it did not meaningfully steal from the main lift result.
