## Coaching assessment
- Bench was the major signal of the day. The prescription was 3x5 @7-7.5, but Ren hit 120kg x5 twice at @9 despite repeatedly contacting the rack. That is a clear strength overshoot, not a failed session.
- The third set at 117.5kg x4 @8 was the right stop. After two @9 sets, forcing rep 5 would have been low-value fatigue. Stopping while still in control protects Saturday deadlift/Larsen and next week's Week 4 top-set test.
- Rack contact is now the main bench technical issue. It likely inflated RPE on the 120kg sets and made the third set less useful as a pure strength read. Next heavy bench should prioritize a slightly lower/farther setup from the hooks before judging load.
- Today's result revises the Week 4 bench expectation upward. The old 120kg x5 target is already achieved under imperfect conditions. The realistic Week 4 top-set target is now 122.5kg x5 @9-9.5, with 125kg x5 available only if warm-ups and setup are excellent.
- Pull-ups were acceptable but still rising. Trying +22.5kg for set 1 was not unreasonable, but +20kg remains the better repeated-set load after a heavy bench overshoot.
- Chest fly again landed above target. 85kg is still too high for low-cost 12s after hard benching if the goal is @7-7.5.
- Triceps and curls repeated the same local-fatigue pattern as last week. 30kg/20kg first sets are usable, but repeated sets quickly become @9.5 unless load drops happen earlier or the starting load is lower.

## Block trajectory impact
- Bench is ahead of the block's secondary objective. Today supports a 122.5kg Week 4 bench benchmark and keeps 125kg as a conditional stretch.
- The cost is that bench and arm/accessory fatigue were higher than prescribed. This is acceptable as a one-day signal because the main lift result was meaningful, but the rest of the week should not chase extra pressing fatigue.

- Bench cue: `set lower, pause, drive back clean`.
- Accessories: choose the load for set 2, not set 1.
