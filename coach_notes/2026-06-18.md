## Coaching assessment
- This was not a clean target-hit bench day, but it was a useful diagnostic session. The programmed target was 1 x 1 @8-8.5, 1 x 3 @8.5-9, and 1 x 3 @7.5-8. The 130kg single and 120kg backoff landed in range; the 125kg triple overshot by about half an RPE point.
- The key bench finding is that pressing strength is not the obvious limiter. Ren hit 125kg x3 @9.5, then still produced 120kg x3 @7.5-8 and a fatigued 127.5kg single around @7.5-8. That sequence points much more toward heavy-bar positioning and self-unrack disruption than toward a lack of strength.
- The 125kg triple was above target, but it served a purpose: it gave specific heavy-bar practice after the 130kg path problem. It should be treated as an intentional exposure, not as the default load-selection model.
- The extra 127.5kg single was the useful closure. It confirmed that a heavy but sub-130 load could be unracked and pressed with better control even after the triples. That is a positive signal for next week if a handoff is used for the 130kg+ work.
- Pull-ups progressed aggressively. +27.5kg is viable for 5s, but the third set at @9 shows it is not yet the automatic repeated load when the target is @8-8.5. +25kg remains the safer default if the goal is clean work without excess fatigue.
- Chest fly repeated the accessory overshoot pattern. 85kg x13 @7 was strong; 85kg x15 @9 went well beyond the planned @7-7.5 stimulus.
- Triceps again confirmed that 30kg is a one-to-two set load, not a repeated third set after the second set reaches @8.5. The final 30kg x9 @9.5 added fatigue without much extra bench-supporting value.
- EZ-bar curls were the cleanest accessory result. 22.5kg x10 @7.5 and @8.5 stayed within the useful range and improved on prior second-set overshoots.

## Block trajectory impact
- Bench remains close to the Strength Block v5 goal of 130kg x3 @9-9.5, but today makes the goal conditional rather than automatic. The strength signal supports an attempt; the technical signal says the attempt should require a handoff and a very quiet setup.
- The 130kg x3 Week 4 attempt is realistic only if the opener-like single is sharp and the heavy bar does not disturb scap/shoulder position out of the rack. Without a handoff or with another self-unrack path issue, 127.5kg x3 or 125kg x3 cleaner than today is the more rational benchmark.
- Today's bench supports the Week 2 assessment: Ren is not mainly failing from low strength. The limiting factor is executing heavy singles/triples without position loss, then stopping before the session becomes a frustration-driven test.
- Upper-back and setup management matter for Saturday deadlift as well. Pull-ups were productive but drifted high, and the recent upper-back/rhomboid history means the next heavy pull should still prioritize setup quality.
- Accessories continue to need earlier drops. The repeated pattern is a strong first set followed by a too-hard second or third set. This is now a block-level fatigue-management theme.

## Technical theme to carry forward
- Bench: `handoff, low touch, press back`.
- Treat the unrack as part of the lift. If the bar leaves the hooks and scap position changes, the rep is already compromised.
- For accessories: once a set reaches the top of target, the next set drops unless the exercise is intentionally being tested.
