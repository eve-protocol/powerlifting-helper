## Coaching assessment
- The session missed its primary objective. Every completed bench work set exceeded the @7 ceiling, and the first over-target set was followed by three more sets rather than a decisive reduction or stop. The extra repetition on set one explains part of the initial @8.5, but 115kg was still too aggressive for calibration on this setup.
- The 110kg sets are not a clean Toronto strength baseline. They followed ten high-effort repetitions at 115kg, including a reracked @9 set, and were performed on a setup that was already causing neck pain. Their @8.5-9 ratings reflect equipment fit, pain, and accumulated fatigue as well as pressing capacity.
- This does not establish a sudden bench-strength loss. Ren previously completed 115kg x6 @7.5 on familiar equipment on 2026-08-14 and 110kg x5 @7-7.5 after the 2026-08-21 benchmark miss. Today's result instead establishes that the Toronto combo-rack bench cannot inherit the Oni-gym load anchor without a full setup recalibration.
- The correct immediate response was to end benching once 115kg x6 overshot, or at latest after 115kg x4 @9 confirmed the mismatch. Continuing to two hard 110kg sets added fatigue without improving calibration.
- The cable crossover produced the intended pec stimulus but overshot on the first set and remained slightly above target after the reduction. On this exact station and seated setup, 30kg total is the next defensible 12-rep starting point; the stack number remains machine-specific.
- Cable triceps showed the size of the new pulley-system difference. Fifty-seven kilograms was below target while 64kg overshot to @9, so approximately 59-60kg is the next starting reference if the same attachment, pulley setting, and technique are used.
- EZ-bar curl ultimately landed in range at 20kg. The additional third set was unnecessary after bench volume had already exceeded plan, but 20kg is a usable local baseline with this bar.
