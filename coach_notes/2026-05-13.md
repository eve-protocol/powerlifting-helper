- Video review: the main technical drift on heavier/later reps was still the expected pattern — hips slightly leading before the chest fully follows. It was not a severe good-morning, but it is the exact limiter this block is trying to improve.
- Ren noticed a useful asymmetry: his left side looked vertically stacked, while the right side showed a forward-on-descent / back-on-ascent arc. Review suggests mild-to-moderate bar/torso yaw or hip/knee asymmetry rather than a simple straight sagittal bar-path problem. Possible contributors are uneven knee tracking, slight hip shift, stance/foot angle asymmetry, or uneven upper-back/bar shelf tension.
- Because the final squat set reached @8, this should be treated as a productive but slightly aggressive exposure, not permission to keep jumping every set. The win is the PR plus mostly acceptable position; the next step is repeating or modestly progressing while making the path cleaner.

- Setup check before descent: equal midfoot pressure, both elbows pulled down evenly, and both knees tracking over the same toe line.
