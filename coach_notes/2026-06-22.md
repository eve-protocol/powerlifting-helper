## Block trajectory impact
- Squat trajectory is positive heading into the Week 4 low-bar benchmark. Paused 142.5kg x2 @7.5 supports the planned 165kg x3 attempt if warm-ups keep chest and hips linked.
- Bench remains on track but should stay setup-gated. Today confirms 112.5-115kg paused strength is there, but the small overshoot reinforces that Thursday should not be turned into a repeated-load fight.
- Upper-back/accessory discipline is still the biggest leak. Pull-ups and the 50kg cable-crunch attempt both show that support work jumps need earlier backoffs when the machine/load increment is coarse.

## Technical theme to carry forward
- Squat: knees forward, then chest and hips together.
- Bench: quiet setup, press back to the rack.
- Accessories: when the next load is a hard jump, own the current load harder instead of forcing the jump.
