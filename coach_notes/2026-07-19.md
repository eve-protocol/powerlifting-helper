## Coaching assessment
- This was a useful but costly heavy deadlift exposure. The floor-strength signal is not the problem. Warm-ups through 180kg moved well, and even the later 182.5kg was reported as fast from the floor. The limiting factor is exactly the active coaching focus: preserving lats/back/hip position through the top half and finishing without the knees staying soft and the chest folding forward.
- The 187.5kg x1 @9.5 should be counted as today's top single, not a clean bridge. It was a made heavy single and a better outcome than repeating the Week 2 mistake of jumping to 190kg after an easy 180kg, but it landed above the top of target and did not show the lockout quality needed to justify a heavier attempt.
- The 182.5kg x1 @10 was the clearest red flag. A lighter second single should have been 8-8.5, but it became a max-effort repeat of the same pattern. That does not mean strength disappeared; it means the first heavy single spent the top-half system and the lockout pattern did not recover between attempts.
- The 175kg double was the best technical correction of the day. The log says @7, and video showed a better hip-through intent than the heavy singles, but the set should be interpreted as technical salvage rather than proof that heavier backoff work was available. Rep 1 showed the cue can work; rep 2 showed the pattern still returns under fatigue.
- Low-bar squat was acceptable but slightly hot on set 1. After a max-effort deadlift second single, 150kg was already the right ceiling. The second set returning to @6 keeps it from being a problem, but this was not the day to explore 152.5-155kg.
- Bench was strong enough but slightly hot on the second set. 120kg x2 @6 was perfect support work. 122.5kg x2 @6.5 is still fine and useful, especially back in a normal gym, but bench should not be used to compensate for the deadlift frustration during mock-meet week.

- The weekly assessment should treat Week 3 as productive but noisy: squat progressed, bench data was partially contaminated by travel-gym setup, and deadlift exposed the exact limiter that must govern mock-meet attempt selection.

## Technical theme to carry forward
- Deadlift: hips through before it slows. The sequence needs to be automatic: bar clears knee, knees finish, hips through. Do not wait until mid-thigh to rescue the lockout.
- For heavy attempts, floor speed is not the decision criterion. The decision criterion is whether the bar stays glued in and the lockout finishes without soft knees/chest folding.
- Squat: after hard pulls, keep light low-bar as position practice only.
