## Coaching assessment
- High bar was the main block-relevant win. Compared with last week's 120kg x6 @7-7.5, today's 127.5kg x5 work is a clear progression while still serving the high-bar developmental purpose.
- The first 122.5kg set was much easier than it looked by video; Ren reported it as around @5. The video slowdown was mostly positional timing, not true effort, which justified moving up.
- The two 127.5kg sets landed correctly for the day: set 2 at @7.5 and set 3 at @8. The last set slightly exceeded the 7-7.5 target, but it was an acceptable final-set overshoot rather than a technical failure.
- Video review across the high-bar sets showed the same technical signature: slight forward drift into the bottom, then a small hips-back / torso re-stack on ascent. This was mild at 122.5kg and more visible on reps 4-5 of the final 127.5kg set.
- The important positive: knees stayed forward enough and the squat did not revert into a full good-morning. The issue is not lack of knees; it is staying stacked over midfoot while keeping those knees.
- Best high-bar cue from today: `knees stay, stand through midfoot` or `knees stay, traps straight up`. Avoid overusing `chest up` because it can make Ren chase vertical torso instead of bar-over-midfoot stacking.
- Paused bench was well chosen. 105kg x4 across three sets with Ren's long 0-1-2-3 pause standard is a strong progression from last week's 102.5kg x5 @8 then 100kg x5 @7-7.5.
- Bench video review of the first set: pauses were legitimate, touch point was consistent around lower sternum / upper abdomen, bar path pressed slightly back toward the rack, and upper back stayed tight. The only small technical note was that leg drive could be more preloaded before the press starts.
- Bench set 3 at @8 was a small overshoot, but acceptable because pause quality and position did not appear to break down.
- Pull-ups were better controlled than the prior Monday. Keeping +20kg across sets produced @7, @7.5, @8 instead of the Week 2 spike to @9.5. Set 3 is still above target, but the load choice was much better.
- Cable crunch stayed low-cost and should not affect the rest of Week 3.

## Technical theme to carry forward
- High bar / squat: keep the current knee travel, but stay stacked over midfoot. The goal is not "more knees"; it is "same knees, less forward drift, less hips-back correction."
- Cue priority: `knees stay, stand through midfoot`.
- Paused bench: keep the long pause strict, but preload leg drive during the pause so the press starts sharper.
