## Coaching assessment
- Deadlift landed poorly as a coaching outcome. The first two 150kg sets were within the written RPE target on paper, but they did not create the intended technical clarity. Pauses were visibly controlled but not consistently owned as a dead-stop position, and the restart remained the confusing part of the lift.
- The major error was the live cue. "Hips through" was intended to prevent a lean-back lockout rescue, but for Ren it overcorrected the movement. On the 140kg set it encouraged an active hip shove rather than a normal continued pull with the bar connected. That made the lighter set much harder and produced a 140kg x5 @9.5 outcome.
- Do not carry forward "hips through" as Ren's main paused-sumo cue. The better standard is simpler: use Ren's normal pull, pause clearly, keep the bar attached, stay tight, and continue the pull. The hips should finish because the pull continues, not because Ren consciously thrusts them forward.
- The 150kg sets do not prove that Ren is too weak for this load. They do show that paused sumo is currently technically expensive and emotionally costly when over-cued. The next exposure should reduce cue complexity rather than chase a bigger correction.
- Incline dumbbell bench was productive but slightly hot. 45kg x8 @7 was right, the second 45kg set reached @8.5, and the drop to 42.5kg restored the target RPE. This supports keeping incline dumbbell bench as a primary bench movement, but 45kg is not yet an automatic across-all-sets load for 8s.
- Lateral raises and rear delts were acceptable but slightly hot on the middle/final work. No issue requiring programming change; just keep the RPE ceiling honest.
