## Coaching assessment
- This was a strong squat outcome. The prescription was 1 x 1 @7.5-8, then 3 x 3 @7.5-8. The top single undershot slightly at @7, and all three 150kg triples landed at @7.5. That is a better-than-planned load result without overshooting effort.
- The key technical win was not just the load. Ren found a better low-bar descent setup: set ribs/torso first, then squat. When he started too tall, the bar/system drifted forward on the way down; when he set the torso angle earlier, the descent stayed more organized.
- The small forward drift is acceptable at today's loads because it did not become a chest dump or hips-back good morning. The ascent on the single and triples stayed controlled enough to support the squat rebuild goal.
- Lower-back sensitivity remains the main warning. Today's result proves strength is present, but the last two heavy low-bar weeks both included Ren noticing more low-back/posterior-chain sensitivity after high-bar was replaced by paused low-bar. This should not be dismissed as only today's four-session lower-body cluster.
- The programming implication is that paused low-bar is specific, but it may be too similar to the rest of the lower-body stress to provide the knee-dominant patterning and lower-back relief that high bar was giving. Next week's setup should consider restoring some high-bar or another protected knee-forward developmental exposure.
- Leg extension was a positive quad signal. 65kg was under target, and 70kg x10 @8 landed correctly.
- T-bar row progressed to 65kg x10 @8 x2. This is useful, but the row must stay strict while lower-back sensitivity is present; do not let it become another hinge.
- Leg curl repeated the familiar overshoot pattern. 30kg opened correctly at @7.5 but became @9 on the second set, and even 25kg finished @8.5. After consecutive deadlift/squat stress, 30kg should not be repeated automatically.

## Block trajectory impact
- Squat trajectory is positive for Strength Block v5. Compared with Week 1's 160kg x1 @7 and 145kg x4 backoffs, today reached 162.5kg x1 @7 and 150kg x3 x3 @7.5. This supports the Week 4 goal of 165kg x3 @9-9.5 if position and back tolerance stay under control.
- The result also supports the active coaching focus: the best reps were produced by organizing the torso and keeping chest/hips connected, not by pushing into a hips-back compensation pattern.
- The caution is accumulated specificity. Sunday through Wednesday created four consecutive lower-body exposures, and paused low-bar plus heavy low-bar may be concentrating too much stress in the same tissues. The next daily assessments should treat lower-back sensitivity as a real modifier.
- Accessories were productive but mixed. Quad and upper-back work progressed; hamstring isolation again showed that posterior-chain fatigue can show up fast after paused deadlift and heavy low-bar.

## Technical theme to carry forward
- Squat: `set torso, then squat`.
- Secondary cue: ribs down before knees unlock; avoid turning this into a visible hinge-first pattern.
- Accessories: repeat loads only if the prior set actually leaves room to stay in range.
