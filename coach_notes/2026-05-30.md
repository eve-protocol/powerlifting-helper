- This was the best deadlift result of the block. The 177.5kg x5 top set landed exactly in the programmed @9-9.5 range and meets the active block goal.
- Video review of 177.5kg showed all five reps locked out, no obvious hitch, and no major hip shoot-up. The final rep was a real grind, but it kept moving and looked like @9.5 rather than a failed max.

## Block trajectory impact
- Deadlift achieved the Week 4 objective: 177.5kg x5 @9-9.5, up from the 170kg x5 @7.5 / 170kg x6 @10 baseline range and much cleaner than the bar-limited Week 3 session.
- This is a meaningful lower-body progress marker because deadlift had been the most stagnant competition lift. It should raise confidence for the next block's 3RM target, but the strength block should still prioritize lockout quality rather than simply chasing a heavier pull.
- Squat already hit the upper load goal earlier this week with 160kg x5, but at @10 and with technical caveats. Deadlift today was cleaner relative to its target than squat was.
- Bench remains strong overall but not as cleanly benchmarked this week. The block's lower-body rebuild goal was still successful because both squat and deadlift moved up in load.

## Technical theme to carry forward
- Deadlift: `ribs down, lats locked, hips through`.
- Keep the lockout quieter. Do not finish by throwing the head/chest up if the same result can be achieved by staying stacked and squeezing hips through.
- Squat: light work after heavy deadlift should remain exactly that: controlled triples, chest with hips, no load chasing.

## Next-time guidance
- Strength block sumo 3RM planning can start from confidence, not damage control. 185kg x3 is realistic if warm-ups show the same setup and the top half stays connected.
- Do not let 177.5kg x5 make the next block careless. The first priority remains preserving lat/back position above the knee and making lockout cleaner.
- Low-bar light slots after deadlift: 140kg triples are validated around @5.5-6 after a hard pull. Use 137.5kg if the target is strictly @5.
- Larsen 4s after deadlift: 110kg is a valid first set around @6, but repeat only if the target permits @7; otherwise use 107.5kg for the second set.
