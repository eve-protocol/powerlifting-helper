- The logged RPE was @10, so it overshot the programmed @9-9.5. This matters: the set proves strength moved, but it was not the cleanest possible expression of the block objective.
- Video review during the session showed the reason. Rep 1-2 were stronger than expected, but rep 2 included a balance loss that spent unnecessary energy. Reps 4-5 then shifted clearly toward hips/back first.
- The result should be counted as a successful benchmark, but not as the stretch outcome. The stretch target was 160kg x5 clean enough that the lower back was not the obvious limiter; this set was strong but still crossed into technical-max territory.
- Backoffs were handled well. Both 140kg sets stayed in the programmed @6.5-7 range, with the second logged at @6.5. Video review showed mild hips-first rise under fatigue, but much less than on the top set and acceptable for backoff work.
- Accessories were mostly disciplined and, importantly, were only two sets each per the active block. The earlier daily assessment had incorrectly listed three accessory sets; the synced program confirms two.
- Leg extension was conservative but useful: 50kg was under target, 55kg landed @6.5 and validated the load for this lower-fatigue two-set version.
- T-bar row was exactly on target at 55kg x12 x2 @7.
- Leg curl repeated the same pattern as prior weeks: 30kg is too high for target-RPE repeated 12s after heavy squatting. Dropping to 25kg was the correct correction, but it also shows 25-27.5kg should be the default range when the target is @6.5-7.

## Technical theme to carry forward
- Main squat cue: `chest with hips`.
- Do not rush the rebound out of the hole. The first inch decides whether the set stays quad/knee-forward enough or turns into a hip/back rescue.
- Backoff success standard: repeated reps should look like today's 140kg sets, not like reps 4-5 of the 160kg top set.
