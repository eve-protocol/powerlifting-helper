## Block trajectory impact
- Very positive for the Week 4 low-bar objective. Compared with Week 2's 142.5kg x6 @7-8, today's 150kg x5 @8 after prior work is a clear strength and confidence progression.
- The Week 4 low-bar target should now be framed as 157.5kg x5 as the realistic first attempt, with 160kg x5 available if the last warm-up and first heavy set show today's same control.
- The technical qualifier remains important: Week 4 should not be judged only by completing 157.5-160kg x5. The win is completing it with less hip-rise / chest-lag than the pre-block 155kg x5 @9 baseline.
- Accessory fatigue was mixed. Rows were good; leg extension and leg curl both overshot. This is not a major problem, but accessories should not become hidden fatigue before the Week 4 squat/deadlift tests.

## Technical theme to carry forward
- Main squat cue: `chest with hips out of the hole`.
- Brace cue: normal belt position, brace low, keep pressure through the first inch.
- Load-management rule: heavier low-bar sets count only when rep 4-5 do not add a bigger hips-first correction than today.
