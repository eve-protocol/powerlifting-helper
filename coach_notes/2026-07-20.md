## Coaching assessment
- The squat outcome was appropriate. 160kg x1 @7.5 hit the programmed target after a fatigue-biased warm-up, and stopping there was the correct choice. Given that 165kg was @8.5 on 2026-07-13 under more normal conditions, there was no value in forcing 165kg today after 150kg already felt heavy.
- Bench was the main overshoot. 132.5kg was selected from the clean 2026-07-09 anchor, and the 120kg x2 warm-up supported it on paper, but it landed @9 instead of @7.5-8. The likely explanation is accumulated systemic fatigue plus no caffeine rather than a true bench regression. Still, this should lower aggression for Week 4 technique work; bench does not need another confidence test before Saturday.
- Deadlift gave useful technical information but cost more fatigue than planned. 170kg was the better opener-rehearsal rep: fast, controlled, and technically cleaner. 180kg was a tired but successful pull, around @8.5, and should not be treated as the ideal opener signal. It confirmed that the narrower stance is viable, but it also confirmed that Ren was fatigue-biased today.
- The narrower deadlift stance is the biggest useful technical finding. Moving the feet 3-4cm narrower appeared to reduce the forced start position, keep knee clearance clean, and preserve lockout. It should be considered seriously for Saturday, provided warm-ups feel good and Achilles/adductors remain quiet.
- The Achilles report matters. One-sided irritation that has increased with heavy squat loading and squat-shoe exposure should not be ignored. It does not require panic from this session alone, but Week 4 should not add extra lower-body volume or aggressive technique experimentation. The remaining squat work should be treated as low-fatigue position practice only.
- Pull-ups after the deadlift were not the priority. +25kg x4 matches recent strength, but because the main-lift work already overshot in fatigue, this accessory should be interpreted as completed support work, not progression evidence.

## Technical theme to carry forward
- Squat: knees forward for the first inch, chest with hips. If Achilles sensation rises during warm-ups, cap the session immediately.
- Bench: trust the setup, not the ego. One clean single is enough.
- Deadlift: use the narrower stance if warm-ups confirm it; lats tight, push the floor long, hips through before the bar slows.
