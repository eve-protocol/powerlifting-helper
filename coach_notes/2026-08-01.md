## Sumo video assessment
- Set 2 and set 3 were reviewed across the source-rate footage. The set had strong speed, stable balance, and little meaningful rep-to-rep deterioration.
- Ren correctly identified that the visible finish is downstream of the setup: he wedges slightly below his loaded pulling position, his torso assumes a mildly curved or “U-shaped” profile, and his hips rise a little before the bar leaves the floor.
- The small hip rise is consistent rather than progressively worsening. It is best interpreted as the body finding its effective start position, not as a major strength failure.
- “Squeeze the armpits into the pockets” improved upper-back organization and bar connection on set 3, but it did not fully remove the hip adjustment.
- A perfectly flat back is not required. The goal is a braced, repeatable spinal position that does not change materially under load.
- Best cue: **Lats tight—pull from there.** Once the slack and lats are set, do not sink the hips farther before initiating the pull.
