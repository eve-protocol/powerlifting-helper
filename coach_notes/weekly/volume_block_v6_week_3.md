## Week 4 objectives for this block
- Low-bar squat: 157.5-160kg x5 @9-9.5, with less hip-rise / chest-lag than the previous 155kg x5 @9 baseline.
- Sumo deadlift: 177.5kg x5 @9-9.5, with cleaner lockout/top-half position than previous heavy rep work.

## Current trajectory
- Overall: still on track for squat and bench, more conditional for deadlift. Week 3 produced a strong heavy low-bar signal and clear bench strength, but the final deadlift day was compromised by the hard chrome ONI bar, grip failures, crowding, and mood.
- Squat: positive. High bar progressed on Monday, heavy low-bar reached 150kg x5 @8 on Wednesday, and Saturday light low-bar was easy at 137.5-140kg x4 after a messy deadlift. The Week 4 157.5kg x5 target is realistic; 160kg remains conditional on position.
- Deadlift: mixed. Tuesday paused deadlift was productive at 160kg x4 @7-7.5, but Saturday regular sumo did not deliver a clean 3x5. Because the limiting factor was the hard chrome ONI bar slipping, not an obvious strength ceiling, the Week 4 goal remains possible but should be judged from normal-bar or strapped warm-ups.
- Bench: ahead but under-controlled. Paused bench 105kg x4s, competition bench 120kg x5 x2 @9, and Larsen 112.5kg x5 @8.5 all show pressing strength. The risk is repeated overshooting from easy openers and accessories, not lack of capacity.
- Accessories: recurring overshoots remain. Pull-ups, incline DB, leg extension, leg curl, chest fly, triceps, curls, and Larsen all had examples of RPE drifting above target.

## Main evidence
- High bar squat: 122.5kg x5, then 127.5kg x5 @7.5 and @8. Productive load progression, but still showing some forward drift / hips-back correction on later reps.
- Paused bench: 105kg x4 @7.5, @7.5, @8 with Ren's long pause standard. Stronger than prior week and useful pressing momentum.
- Paused sumo deadlift: 160kg x4 @7 and @7.5. Position was useful enough to support the deadlift rebuild, but the second set was a small overshoot.
- Heavy low-bar squat: 145kg x5 @7, 147.5kg x5 @7.5, 150kg x5 @8. This is the best squat signal of the block and supports the Week 4 157.5kg first attempt.
- Competition bench: 120kg x5 @9 twice, then 117.5kg x4 @8. Strong but overshot the 7-7.5 target; rack contact likely inflated RPE.
- Sumo deadlift: 170kg x3 @8, 170kg x4 @8.5, 170kg x5 @9 with straps. The first two sets were grip/bar-limited on the hard chrome ONI bar, and the third was completed after fatigue from the failed/slipping attempts.
- Light low-bar after deadlift: 137.5kg x4 and 140kg x4 both below target. This is a positive sign because it stayed easy despite the disrupted deadlift slot.
- Larsen press: 110kg x5 @6.5, then 112.5kg x5 @8.5. The first set was well chosen; the second was an unnecessary overshoot.

## Risks to manage in Week 4
- Deadlift equipment variance is now a real tracking issue. The hard chrome ONI bar should be treated as a different condition from the usual ONI bar. If that bar must be used, straps from the first work set are appropriate and should be logged.
- Do not let the failed/slipping 170kg sets lower the Week 4 deadlift call too much, but also do not ignore the @9 strapped set. Warm-ups and bar choice should decide whether 177.5kg x5 is attempted or whether a cleaner 172.5-175kg x5 is the better benchmark.
- Squat should not be held back because Saturday light low-bar was easy. Week 4 should still test 157.5kg x5 first if warm-ups are normal.
- Bench strength is clearly there, but the pattern of overshooting is now repeated. Week 4 bench should use the planned top set, then disciplined backoffs.
- Accessories and secondary press slots should be loaded from the expected later set, not the easiest first set.

## Week 4 coaching directive
- Squat: plan 157.5kg x5 as the first real benchmark. Move to 160kg only if warm-ups and early reps preserve chest/hip timing better than the old 155kg x5 baseline.
- Deadlift: remove equipment noise before judging strength. Prefer the usual ONI bar. If using the hard chrome ONI bar, use straps for work sets and log it clearly. The key decision is whether lockout/top-half position is clean enough to justify 177.5kg x5.
- Bench: strength is ahead; execution and load discipline matter more than proving capacity. Fix setup/rack contact and avoid reactive jumps after easy openers.
- Accessories: keep them boring. Week 4 is for squat/deadlift/bench benchmarks, not proving isolation loads.

## Bottom line
Week 3 was not clean, but it did not waste the block. Squat is in the best position of the cycle and should be tested confidently next week. Deadlift is still alive but now depends on controlling the bar/grip variable. Bench is strong enough; the main job is to stop secondary work from turning into avoidable fatigue.
