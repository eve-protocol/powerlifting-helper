## Week 1 objectives
- Establish conservative strength-block anchors for 4-rep work after the previous block's benchmark week.
- Keep sumo deadlift progressing toward the Week 4 185kg x3 target while preserving lat/back/hip position through lockout.
- Keep low-bar squat position honest: knees forward early, chest and hips rising together, and no reward for hips-back compensation.
- Keep bench productive without letting singles or secondary pressing overshoot too early in the block.

## Current trajectory
- Overall: on track but demanding. Main lifts produced useful anchors, but Week 1 felt spicy because several loads were exploratory, posterior-chain fatigue showed up midweek, and bench/accessories drifted high before the final day corrected the pattern.
- Deadlift: strongest positive. Paused sumo reached 160-162.5kg x4 early in the week, and regular sumo reached 182.5kg x1 @7.5 plus 170kg x4 @7.5 and @7. This supports the 185kg x3 block goal if lockout stays clean.
- Squat: mixed but usable. Paused squat calibrated around 132.5kg for repeated 4s; heavy low-bar hit 160kg x1 @7 and 145kg x4 backoffs in range, but Ren reported fatigue and concern that the absence of high-bar/knee-forward patterning let low-bar feel more hip/back dominant. Light low-bar on Sunday was clean at 140kg x4 x2 @6.
- Bench: strength is present but load selection was less controlled. Heavy bench's 130kg single overshot to @8.5, and repeating 117.5kg for the second backoff overshot. Pressing is not weak, but Week 2 should use the single to calibrate backoffs instead of proving capacity early.
- Accessories: several useful anchors were found, but the old pattern remains: a first set in range can turn into an overshoot when repeated automatically.

## Risks to carry into Week 2
- Do not let the successful Sunday deadlift erase the fatigue lesson. The Monday session follows a late Sunday lower-body session, not a normal Saturday-to-Monday gap.
- Squat may still need protected knee-forward patterning. The skipped high-bar exposure is not proven to be the cause of Wednesday's less confident low-bar day, but it remains a plausible contributor.
- Bench single selection needs discipline. A strong warm-up does not justify overshooting the target single if it compromises backoff quality.
- Accessories need earlier load drops. The block goal will not be improved by repeated @9 isolation work.

## Week 2 implications
- Monday 2026-06-08 should account for the Sunday-night completion of Day 5. If there is any lower-body fatigue, keep paused squat conservative and prioritize positional quality.
- Deadlift is ahead of schedule enough to progress, but only if the top half remains quieter. Do not chase 185kg triples until warm-ups support it and lockout stays connected.
- Low-bar squat progression should be judged by position, not just by whether 145-150kg backoffs are available.
- Bench should use 127.5kg as the likely cleaner single anchor when the target is @7-7.5 unless warm-ups are exceptionally sharp.

## Verdict
Strength Block v5 Week 1 accomplished the main job: it established real anchors for the block without derailing the lower-body rebuild. Deadlift is in the best position. Squat is strong enough but still needs position protection. Bench is strong but needs stricter load discipline. Week 2 should progress only where the movement quality and RPE caps support it.
