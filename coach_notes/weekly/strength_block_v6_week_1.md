## Weekly verdict
- **Week 1 was completed and preserved the lower-body strength trajectory, but it did not solve pressing consistency or repeat-set fatigue control.** No session needs to be repeated or compensated for.
- Squat remained productive. High-bar volume was completed despite modest RPE drift; primary low bar exceeded the opening ceiling by only 0.5 RPE and was followed by two stable backoffs; Saturday's light low bar finally landed exactly in its low-fatigue role.
- Deadlift remained positive overall. Paused sumo progressed cleanly to 160kg x4 @6.5. Saturday's competition work showed a large response change from 175kg x4 @6.5 to 177.5kg x4 @8.5, but the return to 175kg x4 @7.5 was the correct correction and completed the intended volume.
- Bench was the week's main failure point. Monday paused bench overshot twice, Tuesday incline dumbbells reached @9.5, and Wednesday competition bench stopped at three repetitions rather than completing 122.5kg x4. Saturday Spoto was controlled and useful, but one successful variation session does not erase the fatigue and standardization problem.
- Accessories were completed consistently, but several sets exceeded their role: seated row and cable crunch drifted above range, reverse hyper reached @8 against an @5-6 target, lateral raise reached @9, and preacher curls provoked elbow pain. Friday equipment substitutions corrected future exercise selection, but load discipline still needs enforcement.

## Biggest win
- The lower-body work survived the transition into intensification without losing productive volume. Paused sumo at 160kg x4 @6.5 and stable 150kg low-bar backoffs support continuing the written Week 2 progression rather than reacting defensively to isolated overshoots.

## Biggest limiter
- Pressing fatigue and stop discipline. Across Monday through Wednesday, every pressing session contained work above the intended ceiling, culminating in the incomplete competition-bench opener. The problem is not lack of effort; it is spending effort before the lift that matters most and repeating or escalating after sufficient work has already been obtained.

## Lift-by-lift trend
- **Squat:** Positive but readiness-sensitive. Use squat shoes and reverse as soon as legal depth is clear. Keep the cue: **Knees forward for the first inch; chest and hips rise together.**
- **Bench:** Unresolved and currently the principal realization risk. Preserve meet-order exposure, standardize the bench station/setup, and reduce immediately when the first work set reaches or exceeds the ceiling. Keep the cue: **Settle in the final position, hold the pause, then drive back into the bench.**
- **Incline dumbbell bench:** Force is present, but 50kg/110lb per hand is currently benchmark-level for six rather than a repeatable @7-7.5 volume load. Do not force the Week 2 anchor if available dumbbell jumps cannot match the target.
- **Deadlift:** Positive. The volume-block checkpoint remains passed, paused work remains strong, and Week 1 fours were completed. Set-to-set variability reinforces autoregulation rather than changing the target trajectory. Keep the cue: **Stay connected to the knee, then hips through before the bar slows.**
- **Pull-ups:** Productive. +27.5kg worked cleanly for two Wednesday sets; use repeat-set RPE rather than assuming every day supports the same three-set load.
