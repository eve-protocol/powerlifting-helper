## Week 3 objectives
- Build a credible bridge into Week 4 3RM tests without turning every calibration single into a max.
- Keep sumo deadlift moving toward 185kg x3 while improving the shin/bar/hip setup and top-half lockout position.
- Keep low-bar squat moving toward 165kg x3 while protecting knees-forward timing and lower-back tolerance.
- Keep bench moving toward 130kg x3 while solving heavy-bar setup and handoff/unrack disruption.
- Improve accessory and repeat-load discipline enough that support work does not steal recovery from the Week 4 benchmarks.

## Current trajectory
- Overall: on track for Week 4, with the best lower-body strength signal of the block so far. The Week 4 goals are realistic, but only if execution gates are respected.
- Deadlift: strong positive. Paused sumo reached 167.5kg x3 @7 and @6.5, then regular sumo reached 185kg x1 @9 and 180kg x3 x2 @8/@8.5. This is the clearest evidence yet that 185kg x3 is available if lockout position holds.
- Squat: positive but technique-gated. Paused squat repeated 137.5kg x3 x2 @7.5, heavy low-bar reached 165kg x1 @8.5 and 157.5kg x3 @8.5, and light low-bar finished at 147.5kg x3 @6 after heavy pulls. The strength bridge is there, but heavier triples still want to drift into the old hip/back solution.
- Bench: still on track, but setup-dependent. Heavy bench reached 130kg x1 @8.5 and 125kg x3 @9.5, then Larsen reached 112.5kg x4 @7 and 115kg x4 @8. Strength supports the 130kg x3 goal, but Week 4 needs a clean handoff/setup.
- Accessories: still the main discipline leak. Several first sets were productive, then repeated sets drifted too high: paused bench second set, leg extensions, leg curls, chest fly, triceps, pull-ups, and Larsen second set all showed some version of this pattern.

## Main evidence
- Paused squat: 137.5kg x3 x2 @7.5. Better than Week 2, where 137.5kg was the ceiling and 135kg was the correction.
- Paused bench: 112.5kg x3 @7.5, then @9. First set supports bench progress; second set shows the repeat-load problem.
- Paused sumo: 167.5kg x3 @7 and @6.5. Strong technical and strength evidence, especially with the shins-to-bar / knees-out-forward cue.
- Heavy low-bar: 165kg x1 @8.5, 155kg x3 @8, 157.5kg x3 @8.5, 152.5kg x3 @7.5. Load progressed, but the top triple's final rep was technically loose and lower-back soreness appeared.
- Heavy bench: 130kg x1 @8.5, 125kg x3 @9.5, 120kg x3 @8, and an extra 127.5kg x1 @8. Strength is present; heavy self-unrack and bar path are the constraints.
- Heavy sumo: 185kg x1 @9, 180kg x3 @8, and 180kg x3 @8.5. This is a major positive signal for the 185kg x3 Week 4 goal, despite the backoff set overshooting the planned effort.
- Light low-bar after deadlift: 145kg x3 and 147.5kg x3 @6. Correct light positional work after a hard pull day.
- Larsen press: 112.5kg x4 @7 and 115kg x4 @8. Productive strength signal, with the second set slightly above target.

## Risks to carry into Week 4
- Deadlift lockout position. The new shin-to-bar start is helping, but regular sumo still slows around the thigh and can finish with chest/head-back extension instead of hips meeting the bar.
- Squat rep-3 position. The 157.5kg triple supports the 165kg x3 goal, but the technical limit appeared before the strength limit. Do not let a Week 4 benchmark become an old-pattern good morning.
- Bench setup. 130kg+ strength is there, but self-unrack and bar path disruption can turn a reasonable attempt into a messy one. A handoff should be treated as part of the plan, not a luxury.
- Repeat-load aggression. Week 3 repeatedly showed that a good first set does not guarantee the same load is appropriate for every remaining set.
- Lower-back / heel monitoring. Wednesday's lower-back soreness and transient left heel pain did not stop training, but they are worth checking before Week 4 lower-body decisions.

## Week 4 implications
- Sumo deadlift: 185kg x3 is justified as the target if warm-ups reproduce the shin-to-bar setup and the calibration single does not become a lockout grind. If the top-half position is loose, take 182.5kg or 180kg for a cleaner triple rather than forcing the benchmark.
- Low-bar squat: 165kg x3 is realistic, but it should require clean warm-ups and an acceptable first heavy rep. If hips start beating chest early, 160-162.5kg x3 cleaner is the better block outcome.
- Bench: 130kg x3 is realistic with a handoff and quiet setup. If the 130kg single is path-y or confidence is off, use 127.5kg x3 or 125kg x3 cleaner than Week 3.
- Accessories: Week 4 should not be won or lost on accessories. Use first-set tests when appropriate, but drop earlier once a set reaches the top of the target RPE.

## Verdict
Strength Block v5 Week 3 moved the block forward. Deadlift had the biggest jump, squat built a credible bridge toward 165kg x3, and bench strength remains sufficient for the Week 4 target. The week also made the pass/fail criteria very clear: hit the benchmarks only if the technical constraints hold. For Week 4, Ren should attempt the planned 3RM goals, but the standard is not just load on the bar; it is cleaner execution than the prior heavy-rep patterns.
