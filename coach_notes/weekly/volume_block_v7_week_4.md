# Volume Block v7 - Week 4 Assessment

## Scope
- Block: `volume_block_v7`
- Week assessed: Week 4, 2026-08-17 to 2026-08-22
- Sources: refreshed Boostcamp history, current 12-week history, Week 4 daily coach notes, the Week 3 weekly assessment, active coaching focus, and live assessment-thread reports.
- No Week 4 benchmark videos were supplied. Squat technique, bench failure mechanics, and deadlift hand/bar interaction therefore remain partly unverified.

## Week objective
- Reduce volume enough to reveal whether the first three accumulation weeks transferred into objective five-rep checkpoints.
- Pass 160kg x5 low bar below the prior @10 result, 122.5kg x5 competition bench, 50kg-per-hand x8 incline dumbbell bench @8.5-9, and 180kg x5 sumo @9-9.5 with legal connected lockouts.
- Use benchmark misses to revise later realization targets instead of carrying optimistic projections forward.

## Weekly verdict
- **Week 4 did not realize the block's projected strength outcomes.** Low-bar squat passed its checkpoint, while incline dumbbell bench, competition bench, and sumo deadlift all failed their explicit repetition targets.
- Squat was the only clear realized-strength success: 160kg x5 improved from @10 on 2026-05-27 to @9 despite recent flu and lower reported bodyweight. The technical component remains provisional without video.
- Pressing finished close to, but below, both benchmarks. Incline missed by one rep at the target effort ceiling. Competition bench repeated the exact four-reps-plus-failed-fifth outcome the block was intended to change.
- Deadlift force appeared sufficient for three smooth 180kg repetitions, but the right overhand hand opened painfully on rep four in two attempts. This is a grip-limited competition-lift failure, not a passed posterior-chain benchmark.
- Recovery and test standardization were poor: Ren trained through flu symptoms, benched in the evening on an unfamiliar bench, performed approximately one hour of extra beltless pulling the night before the deadlift benchmark, and repeated failed benchmark loads. These factors reduce diagnostic purity but do not change pass/fail outcomes.
- The programming projections also overestimated reliable realization for bench and deadlift. Week 3 had already shown readiness-sensitive pressing and a deadlift fifth-rep failure pattern; Week 4 did not resolve either limitation.

## Biggest win
- Low-bar squat: 160kg x5 @9 is a genuine effort improvement over the prior 160kg x5 @10 and the clearest evidence that productive volume transferred.

## Biggest limiter
- Reliable expression under benchmark conditions. Bench remained one repetition short, deadlift could not retain the bar beyond three repetitions, and several reduced-fatigue backoffs still ran materially above target.

## Lift-by-lift trend
- **Squat:** Positive but not transformational. Rep strength improved and the checkpoint passed; video is still required before claiming that final-rep chest/hip timing fully met the technical goal.
- **Bench:** Checkpoint failed. Useful volume strength remained—115kg x6 @7.5 in Week 3 and controlled 110kg backoffs after the miss—but 122.5kg x5 was not realized. The future 130kg x3 target requires recalibration under standardized conditions.
- **Incline dumbbell bench:** Close but failed. Fifty kilograms x7 @9 is the best clearly RPE-logged seven at this load, but the programmed eighth repetition was not completed.
- **Deadlift:** Checkpoint failed through grip. The right overhand hand and chalk/bar interface now require direct intervention. Lockout mechanics were not the reported limiter today, but prior Week 3 top-half problems remain unresolved without video.
- **Accessories:** Several movements progressed or held productive loads, but reduced-volume discipline remained inconsistent. The Week 4 Day 5 Spoto sets at @8 and @9.5 were the clearest late-week overshoot.

## Block-goal impact
- The block supports retaining productive volume for squat and general work capacity, but it does not support treating the original bench and deadlift realization projections as reliable.
- Apply the active failure rule immediately: 130kg x3 bench and 187.5kg x3 sumo are conditional calibration outcomes, not fixed strength-block benchmarks. Do not progress to them without submaximal evidence on standardized equipment and, for deadlift, a stable competition grip.
- Do not retest any failed Week 4 five-rep benchmark during the relocation week.

## Week 5 guardrails
- Treat relocation as conditional transition work. Complete written sessions only when logistics, illness recovery, and equipment are normal; omit lower-priority support work rather than compressing sessions.
- Calibrate Toronto bars, benches, racks, dumbbells, cables, and machines before comparing their loads directly with Japan anchors.
- Deadlift: audit right-hand placement, knurl contact, chalk thickness/drying, wrist position, and rep resets before any heavy mixed-grip set. No high-effort five until grip survives controlled submaximal work without pain or opening.
- Bench: standardize rack height, bench geometry, unrack, touch point, and time-of-day expectations. Stop a repeat set when the first set reaches the ceiling.
- Squat: retain the knees-forward initiation and chest/hip timing theme, but do not use the successful benchmark to justify extra relocation-week loading.
- No revenge tests, catch-up sessions, or bonus work.
