## Weekly verdict
- **Week 5 partially achieved its transition objective.** Squat and deadlift calibrated successfully on Toronto equipment, the relocation skip remained a true skip, and no failed Week 4 benchmark was retested. Bench calibration failed because unfamiliar equipment, neck pain, excess repetitions, and repeated RPE overshoots obscured the intended low-fatigue baseline.
- Squat was the most consistently successful lift across the week. High-bar work preserved the shoulders-and-hips timing theme, while low-bar work established that 145kg fives and 140kg fours can land at the intended submaximal efforts on the Toronto setup.
- Deadlift produced the biggest positive new information. After Week 4's painful right-hand opening at 180kg, Ren retained the same mixed grip for twelve controlled repetitions at 170-172.5kg. Video showed minimal speed decay, close bar connection, and no obvious lockout rescue.
- Pressing was the week's clear failure of execution and calibration. Paused bench ran hot early in the week, Day 4 competition bench produced severe neck pain and 20 high-effort repetitions against a 15-repetition low-RPE prescription, and the final Spoto increase overshot to @8.
- The week therefore improves confidence in lower-body transition and Toronto equipment compatibility while increasing the need for bench-specific standardization and stop discipline.

## Biggest win
- Sumo deadlift grip and Toronto-bar calibration: 170kg x4 @6.5, 170kg x4 @6, and 172.5kg x4 @6.5 with secure retention across all twelve repetitions and technically acceptable video-reviewed lockouts.

- **Pull-ups:** Productive. Day 1 missed the written sixth repetitions at +25kg, but Day 3 established +27.5kg for two clean fives @7.5 on Toronto equipment.
