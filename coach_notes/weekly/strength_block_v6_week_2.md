## Weekly verdict
- **Week 2 achieved its primary structural objective: all three competition singles stayed at the written @7-7.5 cap, useful triple work was retained, and no proof attempt replaced the programmed work.**
- Lower-body strength remains viable for Week 3. Deadlift provided the clearest positive signal; squat force was sufficient, but heavy-load movement quality remains unresolved.
- Bench remains the main realization risk. The competition single moved well, but the good-pause 122.5kg triple reached @8.5 and both 120kg backoffs exceeded their intended ceiling. Saturday's strict Spoto success is useful but variation-specific.
- Load discipline improved relative to Week 1 in several sessions: Monday paused bench and pull-ups were reduced after overshoots, Tuesday incline dumbbells were reduced after the heavier set, Wednesday squat backoffs were reduced for movement quality, and Saturday's final deadlift set was reduced. Wednesday's repeated 120kg bench after an @9 backoff remained the major exception.

## Biggest win
- **Deadlift converted well into competition work.** The 182.5kg-target single stayed submaximal, 180kg x3 completed within the programmed top-set range, and every reviewed lockout remained legal-looking without hitching or knee rebend. The final-repetition speed loss still localizes the limiter to knee-to-lockout endurance rather than floor strength.

## Biggest limiter
- **Competition-bench repeat strength and fatigue control.** Fast single-repetition expression again failed to predict repeatable triples. Setup variation contributed, but the repeated pattern across Weeks 1 and 2 means it cannot be dismissed as a one-off station problem.

## Lift-by-lift trend
- **Squat:** Strength is present. High bar progressed and Saturday's light low bar was technically controlled. Wednesday's 160kg triple still produced a visible strategy change under load, compounded by severe quadriceps soreness after three consecutive training days. Week 3's reduced Monday/Tuesday lead-in must be preserved before judging the 167.5kg benchmark.
- **Bench:** Mixed. Paused bench was better controlled than Week 1 and strict Spoto reached 112.5kg x4 twice at target. Competition-bench triples remain the unresolved risk, especially after squat in meet order.
- **Deadlift:** Positive. Paused and competition loads progressed, all Saturday lockouts were legal-looking, and autoregulation improved the final backoff. The top-half slowdown remains the technical limiter.
- **Incline dumbbell bench:** The 50kg x5 set was productive and stopped at the written repetitions. The 55kg x5 benchmark remains a genuine Week 3 test rather than an assumed result.
- **Pull-ups:** +30kg is currently a first-set/top-set load, not an automatic repeat load. Reductions were appropriate.
- **Accessories:** Adherence remained high, but larger stack/dumbbell jumps repeatedly overshot target. Equipment-specific anchors should continue to outrank generic numbers.
