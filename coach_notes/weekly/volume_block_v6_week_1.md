## Week 4 objectives for this block
- Low-bar squat: 157.5-160kg x5 @9-9.5, with less hip-rise / chest-lag than the previous 155kg x5 @9 baseline.
- Sumo deadlift: 177.5kg x5 @9-9.5, with cleaner lockout/top-half position than previous heavy rep work.
- Secondary objective: keep bench progressing without letting bench/pull/accessory overshoots compromise the lower-body rebuild.

## Current trajectory
- Overall: on track after Week 1, but only if Week 2-3 load jumps stay tied to position quality instead of chasing numbers.
- Squat: cautiously on track. 130kg x8 low-bar @7 is a good starting point for a 160kg x5 target, but the positional objective is not yet solved. The best cue found was `chest into the bar out of the hole`; squat shoes and a moderate stance helped. The recurring risk is hips winning the first inch as load/fatigue rises.
- Deadlift: on track. 160kg x6 @6.5-7 landed exactly where Week 1 needed it, and lockouts were clean. The remaining risk is the same first-inch pattern: hips slightly rising before the bar breaks if the wedge/lats are not set first.
- Bench: strength is ahead of the Week 1 target, but RPE discipline was mixed. Competition bench looked strong, but paused bench and Larsen both overshot. Treat bench as strong but do not let it steal recovery from the lower-body goal.
- Accessories: several old accessory loads overshot after peaking-specific detraining. This is expected, not a red flag, but loads should rebuild through clean target sets.

- Light low-bar after deadlift: 130kg x6 plus 130kg x6 @6. Slightly above the intended @5-5.5, and squat shoes looked better than flats.

## Risks to manage in Week 2
- Do not use Week 1 ease as permission to jump aggressively. Week 1 was deliberately low-RPE post-mock-meet re-entry.
- Squat load only counts as progress if knees stay forward through the first inch and chest/hips rise together.
- Deadlift load only counts as progress if the wedge is set before the bar moves and lockout stays clean.
- Bench is strong enough to overshoot easily. Paused bench must be loaded for Ren's 0-1-2-3 pause, not a generic short pause.
- Pull-ups and arm accessories should hold conservative loads until repeated sets stay inside target.

## Week 2 coaching directive
- Squat: progress carefully, but bias toward repeating or making small jumps if later reps revert to hips-first. Main cue: `chest tall / chest into the bar out of the hole` with `knees out and slightly forward`.
- Deadlift: allow moderate progression from Week 1, but cap load if the first inch becomes hips-first. Main cue: `slack out, lats squeezed, push the floor apart, then hips through`.
- Bench: use strength, but stop rewarding overshoots. Paused bench default should be conservative until the long pause stays inside target.
- Accessories: rebuild stable working loads; no need to force pre-peak accessory numbers yet.

## Bottom line
Week 1 did what it needed to do: re-enter volume, establish technical cues, and confirm that deadlift and squat loads are not behind the Week 4 targets. The block is on track, but the success condition is technical: Week 4 should not just produce heavier 5s, it should produce heavier 5s with less squat hip-rise/chest-lag and cleaner deadlift top-half position.
