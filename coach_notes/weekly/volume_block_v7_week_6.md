## Strength Block v6 implications
- Proceed to Strength Block v6 without adding tests or catch-up work.
- Preserve the written competition fours in Week 1; do not use the 180kg x5 success to skip directly to heavy triples or singles.
- Keep squat shoes and the cue: **Knees forward for the first inch; chest and hips rise together.**

- A first set at or above the RPE ceiling triggers an immediate reduction. One reduced diagnostic set is enough; stop when pain or overshoot persists.
- Retain productive volume during intensification rather than allowing today's deadlift success to justify abrupt volume removal.
