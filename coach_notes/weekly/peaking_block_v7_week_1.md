## Week 1 objective
- Start the realization block with competition-specific singles and controlled backoffs while avoiding early peak spending.
- Squat: expose a clean low-bar single around @8 and preserve the rebuild cue under fatigue.
- Bench: practice heavy-bar setup without turning Week 1 into another benchmark attempt.

- Keep support work minimal enough that Week 2 can build.

## Programming audit
- The first week did its job physically, but execution discipline needs tightening. The block is a peak; extra attempts and rep tests add noise that will matter more as Week 2 and Week 3 singles climb.
- Heavy singles should be treated as practice of the exact attempt process. If a cue is being tested, test it before the top single, not during repeated top-load failures.
- The deadlift cue hierarchy is clearer now. "Hips to ceiling" is not a primary heavy-sumo cue for Ren. The better heavy cue is "open knees, hips through, bar glued in." This should be used in the next deadlift exposures.
- Bench needs the same guardrail as deadlift: do not let a handoff or easy support set change the day's purpose. The heavy bench day should develop opener/second-attempt execution, not repeat Week 4's near-miss triple psychology.

## Risks to carry forward
- Deadlift can still look strong until it reaches the knee, then stall if knee/hip clearance and bar-body connection are off.
- Squat fatigue reps still tend toward hips-back / chest-lag. Heavy singles are acceptable only if the first inch out of the hole does not immediately reinforce that pattern.
- Bench confidence is high enough to tempt off-plan work. That is useful only if constrained to the programmed exposure.
- Support work still has a tendency to drift hot; during this peak, accessories should not compete with the main singles.

## Week 2 implications
- Squat: build from 165kg x1 @8. The next heavy single can move up only if the bridge single is clean and the chest/hip timing holds. Keep Monday's light low-bar truly light.
- Bench: use 130kg x2 @9.5 as a cap, not a target. Next heavy bench should prioritize a crisp single in the prescribed range and reliable setup.
- Deadlift: use 185kg x1 @8 as the anchor, but treat the failed attempt as the more important coaching data. Future load increases require clean top-half timing from the first attempt.
- Paused deadlift: keep the pause as a loaded position check. Do not allow the restart to become a lean-back lockout rehearsal.
- Accessories: keep them boring. No extra sets to compensate for undershooting RPE unless the program explicitly calls for it.

## Verdict
Week 1 supports the Peaking Block v7 goals. Strength is present across all three lifts, and the deadlift cue correction is a meaningful step toward the 200kg target. The week also showed the biggest risk for the peak: Ren can turn useful exposures into extra tests when the bar feels good or when a cue fails. Week 2 should keep the same strength trajectory but with cleaner decision rules: one intended heavy exposure, no immediate retries after technical misses, and no extra proof work after the prescribed sets are complete.
