## Block trajectory impact
- Peaking Block v7 starts on track. The day preserved low-bar specificity, reinforced paused bench control, and kept upper-back work productive without adding unnecessary volume.
- Squat still needs the rebuild standard applied even during a peak. The second set drifting to @7 means load should not be chased on light days unless the reps are both easy and position-clean.

- Pull-ups can start from +25kg when target RPE is 7-7.5 and warm-ups are normal, but the backoff rule should stay strict once a set exceeds target.

## Technical theme to carry forward
- Squat: knees forward until the bar moves.
- Bench: hold the pause, press back.
- Pull-ups: chest to bar, no hip kick.
