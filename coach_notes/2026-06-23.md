## Technical theme to carry forward
- Sumo: `hips through, ribs down`.
- Keep the improved pause/start mechanics, then finish by bringing hips to the bar without chasing lockout through the neck and chest.
- Accessories: test the next load when earned, but back off as soon as target RPE is exceeded.
